# Vision Statement Corpus

Every real congregational statement available in this repo, in one place, **sorted by length** so the committee and drafting team can calibrate. Extends [`example_uu_vision_statements.md`](example_uu_vision_statements.md) with material newly extracted from the UUA booklet PDF.

**Provenance:** everything below was extracted from files in `_originals/` — Tricia's Jan 2026 prep work, the "Example UU Vision Statements" doc, and the 98-page UUA *Vision, Mission, and Covenant* booklet (2005). **No web sources** — see [Coverage gap](#coverage-gap) at the bottom.

## Part 2 — Mission statements from real congregations

*Newly extracted from UUA booklet Appendix B (p.70). These are labeled **mission**, not vision — included deliberately, because several read more aspirationally than some statements labeled "vision," which is exactly the confusion this project has to navigate.*

**UU Church of Fort Myers, FL** — bulleted structure:

> The Unitarian Universalist Church of Fort Myers, where religion and reason meet, takes as its mission:
> - To nurture the search for truth and meaning
> - To be a loving, diverse and intergenerational community
> - To minister to each other and the community at large
> - To improve the quality of life by living our values and working for local and universal justice.

**UU Church of Corpus Christi, TX:**

> As a welcoming and accepting, diverse and inquiring religious community, we unite to provide an environment which stimulates a free exchange and exploration of ideas, fosters spiritual and intellectual growth, and serves as a base for active outreach to benefit the world around us.

**UU Community Church, Glen Allen, VA:**

> We are an open and nurturing spiritual community, gathering in love for renewal, learning, and celebration. We are reaching out and living our commitment to social justice and a sustainable environment.

**Church of the Open Door, Chicago, IL** *(non-UU — the most distinctive statement in the whole corpus):*

> Church of the Open Door is a sacred assembly of Black, lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgendered, and heterosexual sisters, brothers, lovers, friends, and allies gathering at the invitation of Christ to seek justice, to extend hospitality, to deepen understanding and wisdom, to affirm our identities, to receive healing and power, and to celebrate the transforming presence of the living God.

> **Why this one is worth reading aloud at a COM meeting:** it names *specific people* rather than abstract qualities. Nothing in the UU corpus above takes that risk. Whatever CUUC drafts, this is the calibration point for "specific enough that only we could have written it."

**Harrisonburg Unitarian Universalists, VA:**

> The purpose of our religious community is to support each other in our various inward journeys toward truth. We will endeavor to do this in an atmosphere of mutual acceptance, openness and friendship. Committed social action on behalf of the local and global communities will be a natural expression of this purpose.

## What the corpus shows

1. **The formula is real.** Most UU statements are variations on *welcoming + spiritual growth + justice*. Read six in a row and they blur. The drafting team's hardest job is not writing well — it is writing something only CUUC could have written.
2. **Short statements survive; long ones get filed.** The 5-to-16-word statements are memorable. The 55-word Needham statement is a *program list* wearing a vision's clothes.
3. **CUUC's current statement is long (34 words) and present-tense** — the two problems Tricia identified, visible side by side with peers here.
4. **Almost none name a specific place, population, or tension.** Palo Alto's "with urgency" and Church of the Open Door's roll-call of people are the exceptions. Specificity is the scarcest quality in the corpus.
5. **The vision/mission line is blurry in practice.** Corpus Christi's "mission" is more future-facing than several statements labeled "vision." Don't over-index on other congregations' labels.

## Coverage gap

**This corpus is limited to offline repo sources.** An attempt to gather statements directly from congregation websites was blocked — this working environment's network policy permits GitHub only, so no congregation page, `uua.org`, or archive could be retrieved. Search-result summaries were rejected as a substitute after they produced two different wordings of the same congregation's statement on consecutive queries.

**Do not let anyone add web-sourced statements to this file without pasting the source page text.** A vision-statement corpus with invented quotes in it is worse than a short one.
