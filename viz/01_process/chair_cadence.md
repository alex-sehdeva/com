# Chair Operating Cadence — September → December 2026

How Alex spends time as COM chair this year. Companion to [`timeline.md`](timeline.md) (what happens when) and [`process_strategy.md`](process_strategy.md) (why). This document is about *the chair's hours*, which are the scarcest resource after COM meeting minutes.

**Honest budget:** this is a volunteer role. The sustainable baseline is **~4–6 hours/week**, with two planned spikes (Oct 18 harvest week, early-November drafting support). Anything the cadence below doesn't cover gets delegated or dropped — that's the point of writing it down.

---

## The weekly rhythm (baseline)

| When | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | 10–15 min | Triage only: email/messages touching the vision project. Answer what takes <2 min, park the rest for the Friday block. Never start real work from the inbox. |
| **Monday** | 30 min | Weekly review: walk `timeline.md` and the open action items. Three questions — what's due this week, what's blocked on someone, what's blocked on me? Update the repo. |
| **Midweek (Tue/Wed)** | 60–90 min | The week's **one big build block** — whatever deliverable this week owns (service plan revision, publicity copy, summary brief, agenda). One deliverable per week; protect this block. |
| **Friday** | 30 min | Communications: nudges to task owners, the standing pings (minister, DLRE liaison, Bill Roberts re: SPARC), and anything parked during the week. Nudge *before* things are late, not after. |
| **Sunday (at church)** | 20–30 min | Face time. Two influencer touchpoints or informal check-ins per Sunday beats any email. From mid-Sept this is also where publicity energy is visible. |

**Meeting weeks add ~2 hours:** agenda + pre-read out ≥5 days before; summary + `decisions_log.md` entry within 48 hours after. The April pattern (agenda → meeting → email summary → log entry) is the template — keep it every time.

**Total:** ~4 hrs baseline weeks, ~6 hrs meeting weeks.

## Rules that keep the cadence honest

1. **One deliverable per week.** The midweek block builds exactly one thing well.
2. **The chair orchestrates; the committee executes.** Every Oct 18 role, publicity task, and influencer touchpoint has a non-Alex owner. Chair time goes to unblocking, not doing.
3. **48-hour capture.** Decisions not in `decisions_log.md` within 48 hours didn't happen (see: the missing May–June record).
4. **Standing external pings** every Friday until resolved: SPARC data (Bill), youth track (DLRE liaison), service partnership (Rev. Danielle), co-chair search.
5. **Protect the spikes** (below) by keeping the weeks around them light — nothing else lands in the Oct 18 harvest week or drafting-support weeks.

## Week-by-week — September to the vote

### Week of Aug 31 (dinner prep)
Build block: dinner packet — printed materials, role-signup sheet, reconstructed May-decisions memory. Send invitation + pre-read; personal notes to minister and DLRE; SPARC status ping to Bill. **Fri Sep 4: kickoff dinner.**

### Week of Sep 7 (follow-through — heaviest non-spike week, ~6 hrs)
Within 48h: dinner summary email + decisions log. Within the week: minister follow-up (service partnership, drafting names), DLRE liaison confirms youth track, co-chair asks go out, drafting-team invitations or open call issued. Build block: mid-Sept meeting agenda + pre-read.

### Week of Sep 14 (mid-September COM meeting)
Meeting: service-plan walkthrough with role-holders, guided-imagery rehearsal #1, drafting team announced, publicity launches. Build block: finalize the Oct 18 order of service with the minister's input.

### Weeks of Sep 21 & Sep 28 (steady state)
Build blocks: supplementary-input form goes live; publicity items ship weekly (owner does the work; chair reviews). Sundays: influencer touchpoints — all 10–15 conversations done **before Oct 4**. Friday pings: SPARC data, childcare/accessibility confirmations, co-chair answer due — nominate at the early-Oct meeting.

### Week of Oct 5 (early-October COM meeting)
Meeting: final logistics review, rehearsal #2, contingency plan (facilitator sick, low turnout, A/V). Build block: day-of run sheet — who stands where when, per the strawman's Day-Of checklist.

### Week of Oct 12 (final week)
Light by design. Build block: materials assembled and staged (cards, covenant half-sheets, dots, newsprint, baskets, crayons). Final publicity push. Brief the minister and all role-holders. **Sun Oct 18: input service.**

### Week of Oct 19 — SPIKE (~8–10 hrs, shared)
The one-week harvest: photograph/transcribe every card, fold in online submissions and youth output, cluster into 4–6 themes plus dissents, write the drafting brief. **Chair coordinates; 3–4 COM members transcribe** — schedule the transcription session *at the early-Oct meeting*, not after the service. Brief in the drafters' hands by **Sun Oct 25**.

### Weeks of Oct 26 & Nov 2 (drafting — protect the drafters)
Chair goes quiet and available: answer drafters' questions same-day, shield them from wordsmithing-by-committee. Build blocks: design the feedback round (newsletter + website + post-service session) and start the follow-through plan skeleton (liturgical use, governance integration, budget ask, ~2031 revisit).

### Weeks of Nov 9 & Nov 16 (feedback round + November COM meeting)
Candidates circulate mid-Nov; run the post-service feedback session. Meeting: review candidates + feedback, decide on a second revision round, work the follow-through plan. Build block: collate feedback for the drafters within 48h of the session.

### Weeks of Nov 23 → mid-Dec (revision and adoption)
Drafters revise; chair coordinates vote logistics with the Board (bundling with SPARC — Bill and Terri). December meeting: final review, **finalize the follow-through plan before the vote**, plan the celebration. **Vote by year end.** Then: write the evaluation/handoff memo for the 2027 chair — that's the last deliverable, not an afterthought.

## Where a co-chair changes this

The cadence above is survivable solo through early October but the **Oct 18 → Dec stretch is designed for two**. Natural split once a co-chair lands:

- **Chair:** drafters, minister/Board relationships, feedback-round design, vote logistics, follow-through plan.
- **Co-chair:** Oct 18 day-of operations, the harvest sprint, supplementary-channel and publicity oversight, decisions-log hygiene.

Until then, the harvest week is the risk to watch: pre-commit the transcription team in September so the spike is shared by default.
